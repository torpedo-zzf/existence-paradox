# World bible and episode outline — proposal, not locked canon

## IP
Working series title: Existence Paradox / 存在悖论. Episode 01: Identity Not Found / 查无此人. A shared near-future world explores identity, memory, artificial intelligence and institutional trust. Each episode should be understandable independently. Names are not cleared for trademarks or storefront conflicts.

## Setting
2042. A civil identity registry links legal identity to services and records. Public-facing systems may report an identity as deceased even when a physical person is present. The system is fallible; its verdict is not proof of consciousness or death. Technology should feel close to everyday life, not like an expensive futuristic metropolis. Ordinary apartments, corridors, clinics and terminals are preferred over large sets.

## Episode 01 central question
What makes you yourself if your records, relationships and memories can all contradict you?

## Provisional characters
- Player: semi-defined adult with a stable civil identity and missing recent memories; avoid fixing name/appearance before casting.
- Lin: close relation, recognizes the registered duplicate; emotionally credible, not automatically hostile.
- Registry investigator: constrained by procedures, knows partial system information.
- Duplicate: apparently living the player's life; motives and ontology remain hidden early.
All roles, names, gender, visual identity and voices require owner approval before asset production.

## Mystery structure (writer-only, proposed)
A controlled identity continuity experiment produced competing claims to the same identity. The duplicate and player may both have legitimate continuity claims. An institution is suppressing discrepancies. The exact mechanism and ultimate responsibility are deliberately unresolved until the owner approves the full outline; do not invent final canon in implementation. Avoid a simple 'AI bad / human good' conclusion.

## Opening slice
1. Player wakes in a familiar apartment. Phone reports a civil death record dated three days earlier.
2. Player can inspect the notice or contact Lin first; different choices change information and trust.
3. The registered counterpart appears through a call or message. Lin's response contradicts the player's memory.
4. Player chooses to preserve evidence or seek a meeting. Stop on two different cliffhangers. Both should raise the same central mystery without resolving it.

## Proposed full episode beats
Act I: identity anomaly and a contradictory witness. Act II: retrieve evidence, investigate a bounded location, compare inconsistent records. Act III: confront duplicate and choose how to use the evidence. Provisional endings: restore identity through official channels; expose the system; reject the binary identity verdict. Exact causality and eligibility require a full branch matrix before production.

## Future anthology candidates (not in MVP)
Human Verification, Day Zero, Memory Testimony, Consciousness Legacy. Reuse world rules and assets only after continuity and rights checks.

## Writing rules
Every clue has a source, reliability and discoverability. Red herrings must remain plausible after the reveal. Avoid arbitrary forced failures; meaningful choices change evidence/trust/routes. Track what each character knows. Player-facing text in Simplified Chinese, internal metadata and identifiers in English. Keep spoiler-bearing canon separate from public marketing material.
